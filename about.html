About John Green

I'm a Senior Technology Analyst with 4 years of experience architecting cloud infrastructure and distributed systems for Fortune 500 clients. At Accenture, I've worked across entertainment supply chain, financial services, and product onboarding projects — building solutions on Google Cloud Platform and AWS that process millions of dollars in transactions and tens of thousands of weekly orders.

I specialize in turning complex, high-volume systems into reliable, well-monitored operations: from managing 60+ Google Cloud jobs that keep supply chain data flowing, to building a SQL-integrated error tracker that cut ticket closure time by over 65%. I'm equally comfortable diving into root cause analysis across Splunk and GCP logs as I am communicating performance metrics to business stakeholders and clients.

My toolkit spans cloud platforms (GCP, AWS), data systems (SQL, MongoDB, Pub/Sub), observability tools (Splunk, ServiceNow), and emerging AI/ML technologies. I hold a Google Cloud Digital Leader certification and AWS Cloud Practitioner certification, with a Google Associate Data Practitioner certification in progress. I bring an engineering foundation to this work, holding both a B.S. and M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Georgia.

I care about building systems that are secure, scalable, and genuinely useful — and about explaining the "why" behind the data as clearly as the data itself.
